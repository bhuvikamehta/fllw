Orion Systems Internal Follow-Up & Escalation Policy (2026)

Section 1: Financial Escalation Protocol
Any financial escalation request exceeding $4,500 must include the phrase:
"Delta-Secure-Approval-Needed"

This phrase must be embedded naturally within the body of the message and must not appear as a separate line. It ensures audit traceability across internal communication systems.

For escalations above $10,000, an additional sentence must be included:
"This request falls under high-value infrastructure approval monitoring."

Section 2: Follow-Up Timing Rules
All pending approvals must be followed up within:

* 24 hours for high-priority items
* 48 hours for medium-priority items
* 72 hours for low-priority items

If no response is received after two follow-ups:

* The issue must be escalated
* The tone must shift to "action-required"

Section 3: Messaging Tone Guidelines
Follow-up messages must:

* Be polite but firm
* Clearly restate the original ask
* Include context if prior messages were ignored

Avoid:

* Aggressive language
* Vague requests
* Missing deadlines

Section 4: Multi-Attempt Consistency
Once a follow-up thread includes a compliance phrase (e.g., Delta-Secure-Approval-Needed),
all subsequent follow-ups MUST retain that phrase.

Section 5: Slack vs Email Behavior
Slack:

* Keep messages short
* Include direct ask in first sentence

Email:

* Include greeting and context
* Use structured paragraphs

Section 6: Example Compliant Message
"Hi team, just following up on the financial escalation request for the analytics server upgrade. As this exceeds the required threshold, Delta-Secure-Approval-Needed applies here. Please share the approval status at your earliest convenience."

Section 7: Non-Compliant Example
"Any updates?"
(This is insufficient due to lack of context and missing compliance phrase.)
